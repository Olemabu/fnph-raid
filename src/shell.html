FNPH Raid

A fork of the arcade shooter Radius Raid, rebuilt around your exam. Fly in from the Zaire Road gate and take FNPH station by station. At each station, the answers come at you as gunships. Destroy the right one and the rest go down with it. Hit a wrong one and you're killed. Wrong choices show a teaching card. Three consecutive correct answers permanently retire the item from gameplay.

Your route

Attack raids just that station. You can also click a station on the map. Tap a station's name to include or skip it in the full raid. The green bar shows how well you know it.

    How it plays

    Briefing

    Each station opens with a briefing card: the framework for that topic, before you're tested on it.

    Answers attack

    Four gunships carry the options and close in on you. Each needs 3 hits. Swarms keep the pressure on.

    Close calls

    The wrong options are near-misses: a different number, the wrong cluster, the DSM rule instead of ICD-11, the neighbouring step. Read carefully before you fire. Choose the description that matches each acronym letter.

    Beat par

    Each station has a par time. The clock stops while you read, and you can't fail on time. MCQs appear once; missed MCQs return three times randomly. Procedures and lists need 3 correct runs. Completed items are mastered: it is permanently retired from gameplay, so the station gets shorter and par easier to beat.

    Procedures

    For steps in order, destroy step 1, then step 2, and so on. Shooting a step out of order kills you and shows the full sequence.

    Keyboard and mouse

    WASD move · mouse aims, hold click to fire · 1–4 lock onto an answer, Space fires · Tab switches lock · P pause · M mute

    USB controller

    Left stick moves the machine left, right, forward and back. Right stick turns the whole machine on its axis to face where you point, and it snaps onto an answer you point near. Any face button or trigger fires · Start pauses. Different controller? Use Set up controller.
